About

NMR-based metabolomics researcher with a PhD from the Centre of BioMedical Research (CBMR), Lucknow. Specializing in clinical and pre-clinical metabolomics for autoimmune inflammatory diseases, biomarker discovery, and therapeutic efficacy evaluation. Over 8 years of research experience with 40+ publications and expertise in biomolecular NMR, multivariate statistical analysis, and disease metabotyping.
